The protocol reads an outcome alphabet that is usually coarser than the raw
click pattern, for instance one that merges every pattern with two or more
clicks into a single multiclick outcome.

\begin{definition}\label{def:coarse}
A \term[A surjection from the raw click patterns onto the declared emitted alphabet, supplied as data by the protocol declaration.]{declared coarse-graining} is a surjection
$\cg:2^{[K]}\to\Vset$ onto the declared emitted alphabet, supplied as data by
the protocol declaration, $[K]$ indexing the \tref{detector ports} of a
\tref{receiver instrument} (Definition~\ref{def:instrument}). With $M_S$ the click-pattern elements of
Theorem~\ref{thm:click-povm} (\eqref{eq:MSdef}), the \term[The measurement the receiver emits, one element per emitted outcome, each the sum of the click-pattern elements of its fibre.]{emitted POVM} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coarse}
  F_v\coloneqq\sum_{S\in\cg^{-1}(v)}M_S ,\qquad v\in\Vset .
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
