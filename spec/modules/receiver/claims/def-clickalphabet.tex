At $K=4$ the sets defined here are the raw, single-click and multiclick sets
of the optical-profile notation
(\repodoc{spec/notation.md#37-optical-profile-symbols}).

\begin{definition}\label{def:clickalphabet}
An \term[The set of detector ports that click in one round.]{outcome} is the set $S\subseteq[K]$ of ports that click; the
\term[The set of all outcomes, the subsets of the K ports.]{raw alphabet} is $2^{[K]}$, in bijection with $\{0,1\}^{K}$ by indicator
vectors. We write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mcsc}
  \SCK\coloneqq\big\{\{k\}:k\in[K]\big\},\qquad
  \MCK\coloneqq\big\{S\subseteq[K]:|S|\ge2\big\}
\end{equation}
for the \term[The outcomes in which exactly one port clicks.]{single-click set} and the \term[The outcomes in which at least two ports click.]{multiclick set}, so that
$2^{[K]}=\{\varnothing\}\sqcup\SCK\sqcup\MCK$, and $\varnothing$ is the
\term[The outcome in which no port clicks.]{no-click outcome}.
\end{definition}
