\begin{definition}\label{def:embed}
A \term[A contraction acting on a chosen subset of the declared modes.]{local element} on a mode subset $\Sigma\subseteq\Modes$ is a matrix
$\Gmat_\Sigma\in\mathbb{C}^{\Sigma\times\Sigma}$ with
$\Gmat_\Sigma^\dagger\Gmat_\Sigma\preceq\id_\Sigma$; $\Sigma$ is its
\term[The set of modes a local element acts on.]{support}. Its \term[The matrix on the whole mode space that acts as a local element on its support and as the identity elsewhere.]{global embedding} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:embed}
  \hat\Gmat\coloneqq\Gmat_\Sigma\oplus\id_{\Modes\setminus\Sigma}
  \ \in\ \mathbb{C}^{\Modes\times\Modes},
\end{equation}
that is, $\hat\Gmat\,\Emd{\Sigma}=\Gmat_\Sigma\Emd{\Sigma}$ and
$\hat\Gmat\,(\id-\Emd{\Sigma})=\id-\Emd{\Sigma}$. A local element with
$\Gmat_\Sigma^\dagger\Gmat_\Sigma=\id_\Sigma$ is a \term[A local element that is an isometry on its support.]{lossless element}.
\end{definition}
