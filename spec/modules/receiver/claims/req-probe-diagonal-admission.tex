The channel grading of the blocks is recovered from the total one only under
a condition on the probe state (Remark~\ref{rem:blockmeaning});
occupation-diagonality is the stronger form of that condition which
validation can check.

\begin{requirement}\label{req:probe-diagonal-admission}
The engine admits a \tref{probe declaration} (input \iface{probe_declaration})
only in the \tref{Gaussian tier} of Definition~\ref{def:probe}, whose states
are \tref{occupation-diagonal} by construction, and refuses a declared probe
state outside it under the \iface{fail_closed} convention.
\end{requirement}
