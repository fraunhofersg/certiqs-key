\begin{definition}\label{def:tolerance}
The \term[a binary64 number, at least zero and below one, that an instance
states in its run configuration]{declared tolerance} is a binary64 number
$\tnum$ with
$0\le\tnum<1$, stated by an instance in its run configuration
(Definition~\ref{def:declared-data}). For a complex matrix $Y$ we write
$\mxn{Y}\coloneqq\max_{i,l}|Y_{il}|$ for its \emph{entrywise maximum modulus}.
The \term[the entrywise maximum moduli of the differences that the predicates
of data name: the Hermiticity defect and the completeness, column, trace and
sum residues]{defects} of data are the entrywise maximum moduli of the
differences
their predicates name:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item the \emph{Hermiticity defect} $\mxn{X-X^{\dagger}}$ of a square matrix
  $X$;
\item the \emph{completeness residue}
  $\max_{s\in\mathsf{S}}\mxn{\sum_{j\in J}a^{(s)}_j-c\,\idon{d_s}}$ of a family of
  \tref[block list]{block lists} $\{a_j\}_{j\in J}$ against a
  \tref{sector map} with blocks $s\in\mathsf{S}$
  (Definition~\ref{def:sector-map}), read with a declared constant $c$;
\item the \emph{column residue} $\max_{l}\big|\sum_{i}P_{il}-1\big|$ of a real
  matrix $P$ read as column-stochastic;
\item the \emph{trace residue} $|\tr X-1|$ of a square matrix $X$ read as
  having unit trace, and the \emph{sum residue} $\big|\sum_{x}p_x-1\big|$ of a
  vector $p$ read as a probability vector.
\end{enumerate}
The sums here are exact sums. A square matrix $X$ passes the
\term[the condition that the least eigenvalue of the Hermitian part of a square
matrix is at least minus the declared tolerance]{positivity floor} at $\tnum$
when
$\lmin\big(\tfrac12(X+X^{\dagger})\big)\ge-\tnum$. Data lie
\term[said of data whose defects are each at most the declared tolerance and
whose blocks read as positive semidefinite each pass the positivity floor at
it]{within the declared tolerance} when each of their defects is at most
$\tnum$ and each of their blocks read as positive semidefinite passes the
positivity floor at $\tnum$.
\end{definition}
