An entrywise enclosure with positive radii of an element whose rank is below
its dimension, and whose support is spanned by no set of coordinate vectors,
contains the element, whose block on the rows the enclosure does not fix at
zero is singular, so no lower bound on a least eigenvalue over it is positive
and it fixes no rank
(Corollary~\ref{cor:least-eigenvalue-over-enclosure}\,(iv)). The projector of
an ideal analyser at an angle whose projector has irrational entries is such
an element. It is often known in another form: as a sum $\sum_m w_m
c_mc_m^{\dagger}$ over columns $c_m$ computed in an enclosing arithmetic, with
exact positive weights $w_m$, as an element is that compresses a projector
through an isometry. Read through its columns, the element keeps the rank the
columns fix (Corollary~\ref{cor:rank-over-factor-enclosure}). The definition
names that reading; it is an addition to a reference, never a replacement of
its entrywise enclosures.

\begin{definition}\label{def:factor-enclosure}
Let $d\ge1$ and $k\ge0$ be integers. A
\term[a matrix of Gaussian-rational centres together with non-negative
rational radii that bound, entry by entry, the modulus of the difference
between a rectangular complex matrix and that centre]{matrix box} of complex
$d\times k$ matrices is a pair $(L_c,t)$ of a $d\times k$ matrix $L_c$ with
entries in $\mathbb{Q}(i)$, its \emph{centre}, and rationals $t_{im}\ge0$,
its \emph{radii}; it \emph{contains} a complex $d\times k$ matrix $L$ when
$|L_{im}-(L_c)_{im}|\le t_{im}$ for all $i,m$. For $k=0$ it contains the one
$d\times0$ matrix.

A
\term[a matrix box of columns together with an exact positive diagonal core,
such that the matrix is the product of a matrix L the box contains, the core
and the adjoint of L]{factor enclosure} of a complex $d\times d$ matrix $X$ is a triple $(L_c,t,W)$ of a
matrix box $(L_c,t)$ of complex $d\times k$ matrices and a diagonal
$k\times k$ matrix $W$ with positive rational diagonal entries, its
\emph{core}, such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:factor-enclosure}
  X\;=\;LWL^{\dagger}\qquad\text{for some }L\text{ that }(L_c,t)\text{ contains}.
\end{equation}
The number $k$ is its \emph{number of columns}, a matrix $L$ that the box
contains is a \emph{factor} of the triple, and the matrices $LWL^{\dagger}$
with $L$ a factor are its \emph{members}, $X$ among them; for $k=0$ the one
member is the zero $d\times d$ matrix. Every member is Hermitian and positive
semidefinite, since $x^{\dagger}LWL^{\dagger}x=\|W^{1/2}L^{\dagger}x\|^{2}$
for every $x\in\mathbb{C}^{d}$.

A \tref{reference} $G$ (Definition~\ref{def:exact-reference}) \emph{carries} a
factor enclosure on a block $G^{(s)}_j$ when it is read, besides the
\tref{entrywise enclosure} $\big(C^{(s)}_j,r^{(s)}_j\big)$ of that block, with
a factor enclosure of $G^{(s)}_j$; the block then satisfies both
\eqref{eq:entrywise-enclosure} and \eqref{eq:factor-enclosure}.
\end{definition}
