One documented procedure produces the snaps an instance applies to what it
declares; a producer that snaps its own emission does so by the procedure its
own claims state. For an operator family read as a measurement the instance
keeps a block whose \tref{reference} (Definition~\ref{def:exact-reference})
is known exactly and already lies on the lattice, and snaps every other block
by a procedure that stays as close to what it starts from as the rule allows
that every element, row and column the data state to be zero stays zero,
which is what the structural support below records
(Requirement~\ref{req:support-safe-snap}); only where that procedure cannot
restore the conditions of the snap does a flagged last resort mix a uniform
family into the elements. The steps are Requirement~\ref{req:snap-reference}
and the shared numerical code (\repodoc{spec/architecture.md}). A snap
relates nothing to the model it replaced beyond its displacement; a run that
holds a reference can read the snapped family only to find points,
evaluating the numbers its key length rests on with the reference in its
place (Definition~\ref{def:evaluated-off-snap}), and where it does not, it
rests on the snap (Requirement~\ref{req:snap-reference}\,(iii)).

\begin{definition}\label{def:snap}
Let $D$ be data a declarer is about to declare, a producer's exact
construction of what it emits (Definition~\ref{def:declared-data}), or a
\tref{reference} of an operator family (Definition~\ref{def:exact-reference})
--- a family of block lists against a sector map
(Definition~\ref{def:sector-map}) over an alphabet with a declared order, a
matrix or a vector --- read with the conditions its declaration states of it,
and let $k$ be a \tref{lattice exponent}
(Definition~\ref{def:binary64}\,(iv)). For a family read as a measurement,
those conditions are exact admissibility with its declared constant
(Definition~\ref{def:exact-admissibility}); every sum they read is read in its
declared order (Definition~\ref{def:declared-order}). A \term[at a lattice
exponent k, data of the same shape as the given data, over the same alphabet
in the same order, with every entry a lattice value at k, that satisfy exactly
the conditions the declaration of the given data states]{snap} of $D$ at
$k$ is data $D'$ of the same shape, over the same alphabet in the same order,
every entry of which is a \tref{lattice value} at $k$, and which satisfy those
conditions exactly. Its \emph{displacement} is the largest entrywise modulus
of $D'-D$, taken over every block of every element
(Definition~\ref{def:tolerance}), with $D$ replaced by its centres where $D$
is a reference.

An entry of $D$ is
\term[said of an entry of data known to be exactly zero: for data given as
numbers, an entry that is the number zero; for a reference, an entry whose
centre and radius are both zero; and no other entry]{structurally zero} when
it is known to be exactly zero, which is the following and nothing else: where
$D$ is given as numbers, an entry that is the number $0$; where $D$ is a
reference, an entry whose centre and radius are both $0$. An entry of a
reference whose centre is $0$ and whose radius is not is not structurally
zero. The
\term[of a block of an element: the set of indices whose row or column holds
an entry that is not structurally zero]{structural support} of a block
$D^{(s)}_j$ is the set of indices $i$ such that row $i$ or column $i$ of
$D^{(s)}_j$ holds an entry that is not structurally zero. A row, a column or
an element all of whose entries are structurally zero is structurally zero,
and an index lies outside the structural support exactly when both its row
and its column are. For a reference, whose centres are Hermitian and whose
radii are symmetric (Definition~\ref{def:exact-reference}), row $i$ of a block
is structurally zero exactly when column $i$ is.

Where $D$ is an operator family read as a measurement against a sector map
with blocks $s\in\mathsf{S}$, over an alphabet $J$, a block $s$ of a snap
$F=D'$ of $D$ is
\term[a block of a snap of an operator family read as a measurement on which
the snap is decided exactly to equal the data it is made from]{kept} when
$F^{(s)}_j=D^{(s)}_j$ is decided exactly for every $j\in J$, and
\term[a block of a snap of an operator family read as a measurement that is
not kept: the snap is not decided exactly to equal on it the data it is made
from]{moved} otherwise. Here $D$ on block $s$ is the data the snap is made
from: the centres of an \tref{exact reference} of the block where the snap
runs on them, a producer's exact construction, or the data as they stand
where the declarer states them with no model behind them. Where the
declarer computes the data of the block from a model of it and the snap
runs on that evaluation, the data the snap is made from are the model, of
which the evaluation is no exact presentation, so no exact decision equates
the snap with them and the block is moved, even where the snap leaves the
evaluation unchanged. A snap with no moved block changes nothing.

The definition fixes what a snap produces and not how. The displacement is a
record of the move, and it is the whole of what relates $D'$ to $D$ here.
\end{definition}
