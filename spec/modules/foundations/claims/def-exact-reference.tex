A family declared on a lattice is a family of dyadic rationals; the model it
is declared from need not be one. Photon-number blocks written in the
orthonormal occupation basis carry factors $1/\sqrt{n}$ even when every
parameter of the model is rational. A polarisation analyser whose analysed
state is $\cos\alpha\ket{H}+\sin\alpha\ket{V}$ has on the one-photon block
the entries $\cos^2\alpha$, $\cos\alpha\sin\alpha$ and $\sin^2\alpha$ in the
$H$, $V$ basis, which are all rational exactly when $\cos\alpha=0$ or
$\tan\alpha$ is rational; at $\alpha=\pi/4$ the cosine is irrational and
every entry is $1/2$, while at $\alpha=\pi/8$ the entries are irrational. A decision
about such a model is still a decision on rationals when each of its entries
is known to lie in a rational box around a Gaussian-rational centre, and the
definition reads the model through such boxes. A block of the form
$D^{-1}SD^{-1}$, with $S$ Gaussian-rational and $D$ the diagonal matrix of
square roots of positive rationals, receives one from rational bounds on
those square roots (Lemma~\ref{lem:enclosure-psd}\,(ii)), and an entry known
exactly is enclosed with radius zero. A model whose entries are all Gaussian
rationals is the case in which every radius is zero.

\begin{definition}\label{def:exact-reference}
Let $\mathfrak{m}$ be a \tref{sector map} with blocks $s\in\mathsf{S}$ of
dimensions $d_s$ (Definition~\ref{def:sector-map}), let $J$ be a finite
non-empty alphabet with a \tref{declared order}, and let $c>0$ be a
\tref{declared value} (Definition~\ref{def:binary64}\,(iii)). A
\term[a Gaussian-rational Hermitian centre together with symmetric
non-negative rational radii that bound, entry by entry, the modulus of the difference between a
matrix and that centre]{entrywise enclosure} of a complex $d\times d$ matrix
$X$ is a pair $(X_c,r)$ of a Hermitian $d\times d$ matrix $X_c$ with entries
in $\mathbb{Q}(i)=\{x+iy:x,y\in\mathbb{Q}\}$, its \emph{centre}, and
rationals $r_{il}=r_{li}\ge0$, its \emph{radii}, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:entrywise-enclosure}
  \big|X_{il}-(X_c)_{il}\big|\;\le\;r_{il}\qquad\text{for all }i,l .
\end{equation}
A \term[a family of Hermitian positive semidefinite block lists complete at
the constant on every block, read together with an entrywise enclosure of
every block of every element]{reference} against $\mathfrak{m}$ over $J$
with constant $c$ is a family $G=\{G_j\}_{j\in J}$ of block lists against
$\mathfrak{m}$ whose blocks are Hermitian and positive semidefinite, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reference-complete}
  \sum_{j\in J}G^{(s)}_j\;=\;c\,\idon{d_s}\qquad\text{for every }s\in\mathsf{S},
\end{equation}
read together with an entrywise enclosure $\big(C^{(s)}_j,r^{(s)}_j\big)$ of
every block $G^{(s)}_j$. It is an
\term[a reference all of whose radii are zero, so that each block is its
Gaussian-rational centre]{exact reference} when every radius is $0$; then
$G^{(s)}_j=C^{(s)}_j$ has entries in $\mathbb{Q}(i)$, and its Hermiticity,
positive semidefiniteness and completeness are properties of finitely many
Gaussian rationals.

For a Hermitian $d_s\times d_s$ matrix $Y$ with entries in $\mathbb{Q}(i)$,
the pair $\big(C^{(s)}_j-Y,\,r^{(s)}_j\big)$ is an entrywise enclosure of
$G^{(s)}_j-Y$, since the two differences in \eqref{eq:entrywise-enclosure}
are equal. A reference is an object of its own beside a family declared from
it: the entries of the declared family are declared values, and each denotes
exactly the dyadic rational it is (Definition~\ref{def:binary64}\,(iii)).
\end{definition}
