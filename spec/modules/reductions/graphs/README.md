## Module summary

Summaries explain purpose and content. They are non-normative; the claim statements, proofs and declared assumptions remain authoritative.

**Non-normative summary.** This contract replaces a physical receiver, and in trusted\-source mode a physical source as well, by finite models a security analysis can run on, and states exactly what each replacement transfers\. A squashing relation asks that the adjoint of a channel pull every target POVM element back to the physical one, together with carried operators that move device facts into the squashed model; because the physical measurement channel then factors through the squashing channel, the physical and the squashed protocol have the same output state\. The core module has four main results, in the order the contract states them\. Security of the squashed model over analysed inputs that contain the squashed image of every physical input transfers to the physical protocol with the same ε, for every criterion \([Theorem “Security transfer from a constrained squashed model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer)\), and the attack\-set licence places every squashed physical attack in the admissible set the consuming key\-rate engine restricts to, whenever each carried operator's pull\-back is bounded above or below by, or equal to, a multiple of the identity, a spectral certificate \([Theorem “The attack\-set licence”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:licence)\)\. In trusted\-source mode the source replacement, which exchanges the specified signal ensemble for its virtual one, and the squashing reduction compose, in either order, into one exact operational reduction whose image lies in the admissible attack set \([Theorem “The two\-sided reduction in trusted\-source mode”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:two-sided)\), and the target a reduction instance emits is exactly admissible and squashes the declared model with no error term \([Theorem “Exact emission of a reduction instance”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:emit)\)\. The two submodules, wpfss \(flag\-state and weight\-preserving squashers\) and domination \(the link that relates a dominated family to the one dominating it, kept for a declared device weight\), follow the core as parts of their own and add main results, listed with these four in the front matter\. Every construction is a reduction instance, emitted as a reduction chain of typed links that each carry their channel witness, and every condition on declared numbers is decided exactly, every declared and emitted operator entry and declared real scalar lying on a declared lattice of binary64 \(double\-precision\) values; where the emission has to move an instance's datum, exact arithmetic having left that lattice, the emission theorem is about the moved, declared model, which nothing proved relates to the handed one; a moved class is disclosed, and a run that reads it rests on the premise that what it concludes from the emission holds of the declared model, as it does where the device measures that model\. Where the declaring instance hands a reference of its measurement beside its snap, the same construction run on the reference, with no normalizer, is emitted beside the artefact as its reference companion \([Corollary “The reference companion of a reduction instance”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:reference-companion)\)\. The supplier's device facts, the independence of the measuring party's private setting and the round structure are assumptions the caller carries, while the soundness of the emitted constraints is a conclusion of the chain, emission and licence theorems and not an assumption; the adversary's registers are finite\-dimensional, as the round model's are, and the consuming engine carries the security statement to an adversary whose memory has any dimension \([Corollary “Security of the protocol and its net key”](../../meat-core/contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-meat-core/cor:composed-security)\)\.

## Part I: Mathematics

### 1 Squashing relations and their statistics

[Dependency view](group-01-squashing-relations-and-their-statistics.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** A squashing relation replaces the physical measurement on Bob's system by a target measurement on a finite system, through a channel whose adjoint pulls every target element back to the physical one, and a tuple of carried operators travels along with prescribed pull\-backs\. The relation is equivalent to agreement of all statistics, side system included \([Lemma “Squashing as statistics equivalence”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:statistics)\), and it turns a device fact about a carried operator's pull\-back into an operator constraint on every state the squashing channel produces, the fact being the constraint's certificate \([Corollary “Pull\-back of operator bounds”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:pullback)\)\. The setting comes first, a separable physical system with finite declared targets and side registers \([Definition “The system setting of the reductions”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:system-setting)\), and with it the infinite\-dimensional bookkeeping behind adjoints and tensor channels \([Lemma “Separation and tensor adjoints in the infinite\-dimensional case”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:sep)\); the section ends with the measurement channel, through which the relation becomes a factorisation of channels \([Lemma “Measurement\-channel factorization”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:msmfact)\)\.

- 1.1 [`reductions/def:system-setting`](../claims/def-system-setting.tex): The system setting of the reductions ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:system-setting))

  **Non-normative summary.** The physical system Bob's receiver acts on has a separable Hilbert space, possibly infinite\-dimensional, while every target space and every side register other than the adversary's is finite\-dimensional with declared dimensions\. The squashing relations and their transport are stated in it\.

- 1.2 [`reductions/lem:sep`](../claims/lem-sep.tex): Separation and tensor adjoints in the infinite-dimensional case ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:sep))

  **Non-normative summary.** States separate bounded operators on a separable space; trace\-preserving completely positive maps of any output dimension have unital completely positive adjoints; and tensor products of channels into finite targets, or from finite inputs, are channels\. Transport arguments rest on this\.

- 1.3 [`reductions/def:squashing`](../claims/def-squashing.tex): Squashing relation ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:squashing))

  **Non-normative summary.** A squashing relation asks that the adjoint of a channel pull every target POVM element back to the physical one, and every carried operator back to its prescribed physical partner\. The carried operators let one reduction move device facts into the squashed model as well as outcome statistics\.

- 1.4 [`reductions/lem:statistics`](../claims/lem-statistics.tex): Squashing as statistics equivalence ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:statistics))

  **Non-normative summary.** A squashing relation holds exactly when the physical and the squashed model give the same statistics for every POVM element and carried operator, correlations with a finite side system included\. Two channels realising one relation therefore have adjoints that agree on the span of those operators\.

- 1.5 [`reductions/rem:dictionary`](../claims/rem-dictionary.tex): Direction of the squashing symbol in the literature ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:dictionary))

  **Non-normative summary.** The physical POVM stands on the left of the squashing symbol, as in the squashing\-model literature, while the measurement\-theory literature writes the same relation the other way round\. The direction is to be checked before a statement is imported from there\.

- 1.6 [`reductions/rem:carried-operators`](../claims/rem-carried-operators.tex): Carried operators and the image of the squashing channel ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:carried-operators))

  **Non-normative summary.** The pull\-back identity of the POVM alone does not confine a squashed analysis to the image of the physical inputs\. Operators with a prescribed pull\-back carry device facts across, which is why the squashing relation carries a tuple of them from the start\.

- 1.7 [`reductions/cor:pullback`](../claims/cor-pullback.tex): Pull-back of operator bounds ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:pullback))

  **Non-normative summary.** A carried operator's partial expectation on a squashed state equals its pull\-back's on the physical input, as an operator on the side system\. An identity, positivity or spectral bound on the pull\-back therefore becomes an operator constraint on every state the squashing channel produces\.

- 1.8 [`reductions/def:certificate`](../claims/def-certificate.tex): Certificates of transported constraints ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:certificate))

  **Non-normative summary.** The certificate of a transported constraint is the hypothesis that makes it hold: for a case of [Corollary “Pull\-back of operator bounds”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:pullback) an operator fact about the device and the channel, valid whatever the input, and for the assumed case of [Corollary “Sound constraints from transported operators”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:transported) an assumption on the input states\.

- 1.9 [`reductions/rem:notchannel`](../claims/rem-notchannel.tex): Reductions that are not squashings ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:notchannel))

  **Non-normative summary.** The universal squash model establishes a statistical equivalence and cutoff\-based dimension reduction controls the effect of a cutoff, neither by exhibiting a squashing channel\. Without such a channel the output\-state equality behind [Theorem “Security transfer from a constrained squashed model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer) is not available\.

- 1.10 [`reductions/def:msm`](../claims/def-msm.tex): Measurement channel ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:msm))

  **Non-normative summary.** The measurement channel of a POVM takes a quantum input to the classical register holding its outcome\. It separates Bob's measurement from the protocol's later processing\.

- 1.11 [`reductions/lem:msmfact`](../claims/lem-msmfact.tex): Measurement-channel factorization ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:msmfact))

  **Non-normative summary.** A POVM squashes to a target via a channel exactly when its measurement channel is the target's measurement channel after that channel\. This is the channel identity the protocol\-level argument inserts round by round\.


### 2 Protocols, security criteria and exact operational reductions

[Dependency view](group-02-protocols-security-criteria-and-exact-operational-reductions.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** The protocols of this contract pass each round's measured system through one measurement channel and process only its classical outcome afterwards\. A security criterion is any family of functions of the protocol's final output together with the adversary's system, and an exact operational reduction is a map of attack strategies under which every strategy and its image produce the same full output\. Such a map transfers every bound on every criterion with the same ε, and reductions restrict, enlarge and compose \([Lemma “Transfer and composition of exact operational reductions”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:opreduction-transfer)\)\. The section closes with the reason both reductions of this contract are exact rather than approximate\.

- 2.1 [`reductions/def:protocol`](../claims/def-protocol.tex): Measurement-factorizing n-round protocol ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:protocol))

  **Non-normative summary.** An n\-round protocol passes each round's measured system, held by the measuring parties, through one measurement channel, the same in every round, and no later processing touches that system\. The squashed protocol is the same channel with the target POVM in place of the physical one\.

- 2.2 [`reductions/def:criterion`](../claims/def-criterion.tex): Security criterion ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:criterion))

  **Non-normative summary.** A security criterion is any family of functions, one per admissible adversary system, of the joint state of the protocol's output registers and that system\. Equality of those states therefore transfers every criterion at once, with no proof for its particular formula\.

- 2.3 [`reductions/def:opreduction`](../claims/def-opreduction.tex): Exact operational reduction ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:opreduction))

  **Non-normative summary.** A protocol presentation is a set of attack strategies with the full output each produces; an exact operational reduction maps strategies between two presentations over the same registers so that every strategy and its image give the same output\. Bounds carry back with no property of the criterion\.

- 2.4 [`reductions/lem:opreduction-transfer`](../claims/lem-opreduction-transfer.tex): Transfer and composition of exact operational reductions ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:opreduction-transfer))

  **Non-normative summary.** An exact operational reduction carries every bound on every function of the full output from the analysed to the physical strategy set with the same ε, and reductions restrict, enlarge and compose\. A bound over an intersection of analysed sets needs one map into all of them\.

- 2.5 [`reductions/rem:why-exact`](../claims/rem-why-exact.tex): Exactness of the reductions and generality of the transfer ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:why-exact))

  **Non-normative summary.** Equality of full outputs lets one argument transfer every criterion with the same ε, while a distance bound would need a modulus of continuity no criterion is given\. Every reduction and link of this contract, the domination link included, is exact\.


### 3 Output-state equality and security transfer

[Dependency view](group-03-output-state-equality-and-security-transfer.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Replacing each round's physical measurement by the squashing channel followed by the target measurement leaves the protocol's whole output state unchanged \([Theorem “Equality of the physical and squashed output states”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:factorization)\), also against adaptive attack sequences in either source mode \([Corollary “Output\-state equality against adaptive attack sequences”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:sequential)\)\. The main result, [Theorem “Security transfer from a constrained squashed model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer), is that security of the squashed protocol over analysed inputs that contain the squashed image of every physical input transfers to the physical protocol with the same ε, for every criterion\. A constraint is sound when every reachable squashed state satisfies it; sound constraints may be intersected when they are sound for one common channel and one family of physical inputs, and carried operators supply them \([Corollary “Imposing and intersecting sound constraints”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:sound), [Corollary “Sound constraints from transported operators”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:transported)\)\. The remarks give the variants of the factorisation, the criteria that transfer, and the standard way a transplanted operator identity makes a constraint unsound\.

- 3.1 [`reductions/thm:factorization`](../claims/thm-factorization.tex): Equality of the physical and squashed output states ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:factorization))

  **Non-normative summary.** Replacing each round's physical measurement by the squashing channel followed by the target measurement leaves the protocol's whole output state unchanged\. Every security criterion therefore takes the same value on the physical and the squashed protocol, with no error term\.

- 3.2 [`reductions/def:roundmodel`](../claims/def-roundmodel.tex): Round model and attack sequences, typed by source mode ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:roundmodel))

  **Non-normative summary.** An attack sequence is the round model's, at the measured system; its squashed sequence applies the squashing channel to the measured system alone, leaving Alice's system and Eve's memory untouched\. Where several parties measure, that channel is a product of one channel per party\.

- 3.3 [`reductions/cor:sequential`](../claims/cor-sequential.tex): Output-state equality against adaptive attack sequences ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:sequential))

  **Non-normative summary.** In either source mode, the physical protocol under an attack sequence and the squashed protocol under its squashed sequence have the same output state\. Security against squashed attack families containing every squashed physical attack therefore transfers with the same ε\.

- 3.4 [`reductions/prop:platform`](../claims/prop-platform.tex): Measurement factorization of the platform's protocol channel ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:platform))

  **Non-normative summary.** For a well\-formed declaration with one sector on A, one measured system, POVM form, two messages neither reading the other and no party\-scoped entry, the round channel and its physical counterpart factor as in [Definition “Measurement\-factorizing n\-round protocol”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:protocol)\. A squashing relation carries the one output to the other\.

- 3.5 [`reductions/rem:platform-published`](../claims/rem-platform-published.tex): Published forms of the sequential reduction ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:platform-published))

  **Non-normative summary.** The reduction of [Corollary “Output\-state equality against adaptive attack sequences”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:sequential) and [Proposition “Measurement factorization of the platform's protocol channel”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:platform) is published for narrower protocol models, with a restricted or an unrestricted squashed attack set, and in a non\-adaptive single\-channel version\. No proof here uses those statements\.

- 3.6 [`reductions/rem:variants`](../claims/rem-variants.tex): Variants of the factorization ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:variants))

  **Non-normative summary.** The factorization holds verbatim for round\-dependent squashers, for a private per\-round setting squashed by one common channel, and for a correlated n\-round device\. A setting\-dependent squashing channel does not fit, and hands the setting register to the adversary\.

- 3.7 [`reductions/rem:coherent`](../claims/rem-coherent.tex): Coherent attacks covered by the output-state equality ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:coherent))

  **Non-normative summary.** The output\-state equality holds for an arbitrary adversary input and an arbitrary attack sequence, so it covers an adversary entangling all n rounds\. The tensor power is a hypothesis about Bob's device, not about the adversary\.

- 3.8 [`reductions/rem:criteria`](../claims/rem-criteria.tex): Correctness, secrecy and completeness as criteria ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:criteria))

  **Non-normative summary.** Correctness and secrecy in the composable sense are functions of the output state, so equality of output states transfers them\. Completeness compares the protocol against an honest reference channel, is not of that form and is not transferred\.

- 3.9 [`reductions/rem:completeness`](../claims/rem-completeness.tex): Completeness statements in the physical and squashed models ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:completeness))

  **Non-normative summary.** A completeness criterion compares the protocol against an honest reference channel and is outside the form that transfers\. No gap opens: the squashed model reproduces the physical statistics exactly, so a completeness statement computed in one model holds in the other\.

- 3.10 [`reductions/def:sound`](../claims/def-sound.tex): Reachable states and sound constraints ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:sound))

  **Non-normative summary.** A squashed state is reachable when it is the squashed image of a physical input, and a family of squashed states, or a constraint, is sound when it contains every reachable state\. Soundness is what keeps a constrained analysis from excluding a physical attack\.

- 3.11 [`reductions/thm:transfer`](../claims/thm-transfer.tex): Security transfer from a constrained squashed model ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer))

  **Non-normative summary.** If the analysed squashed inputs contain the squashed image of every physical input, a bound ε on any criterion of the output state of the squashed protocol holds for the physical protocol with the same ε\. No property of the criterion is used\.

- 3.12 [`reductions/cor:sound`](../claims/cor-sound.tex): Imposing and intersecting sound constraints ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:sound))

  **Non-normative summary.** Analysed squashed inputs satisfy the inclusion of [Theorem “Security transfer from a constrained squashed model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer) exactly when they form a sound family, and any intersection of sound families for one channel and one physical input family is sound\. Enlarging a sound family costs tightness, never soundness\.

- 3.13 [`reductions/cor:transported`](../claims/cor-transported.tex): Sound constraints from transported operators ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:transported))

  **Non-normative summary.** On the n\-round relation, a transported operator tested on a squashed image gives what its pull\-back gives on the physical input\. Certified operator facts, and bounds assumed on the physical input family, therefore give sound constraints on the squashed states\.

- 3.14 [`reductions/rem:unsound`](../claims/rem-unsound.tex): The standard way to make a squashed constraint unsound ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:unsound))

  **Non-normative summary.** Imposed as an operator identity on a target that fails it, the physical class\-marginal identity would exclude reachable states and void [Theorem “Security transfer from a constrained squashed model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer)\. Its sound squashed form is the expectation identity, whose certificate is available exactly where the reduction instance discharges it\.

- 3.15 [`reductions/rem:channel-choice`](../claims/rem-channel-choice.tex): Soundness relative to the channel, fixed by the carried tuple ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:channel-choice))

  **Non-normative summary.** Channels realizing one squashing relation can have different reachable sets, so constraints sound for different channels may not be combined\. A constraint built from an operator in the carried tuple has the same pull\-back for every realizing channel\.


### 4 Operations on squashing relations

[Dependency view](group-04-operations-on-squashing-relations.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Squashing relations compose along a pre\-order and survive common classical post\-processing, a privately drawn setting under one common channel, mixtures and tensor products\. Pinching onto the sectors of a block\-diagonal target is itself a squashing; where the carried operators are block diagonal too, the squashed analysis may be restricted to block\-diagonal states for the pinched channel \([Corollary “Restriction to block\-diagonal squashed states”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:blockdiag)\)\. A private setting with declared class weights enters as part of a joint outcome, the weight inside the element, and the joint family is defined once here \([Definition “Joint alphabet, classes and the joint family of a private setting”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:joint-family)\)\. Two necessary conditions can show a declared relation impossible, and both are decided exactly on the declared numbers \([Lemma “Necessary conditions for a squashing relation”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:obstructions), [Remark “Exact decision of the necessary conditions”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:obstructiondecide)\)\.

- 4.1 [`reductions/prop:preorder`](../claims/prop-preorder.tex): The squashing relation as a pre-order ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:preorder))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every POVM squashes to itself through the identity channel, and two successive squashing relations compose into one, their carried operator tuples included\. A chain of squashers is therefore again a squashing relation that keeps its constraint information\.

- 4.2 [`reductions/cor:monotone`](../claims/cor-monotone.tex): Monotonicity of the worst-case criterion along the pre-order ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:monotone))

  **Non-normative summary.** If one target squashes to a cleaner one, its worst\-case criterion over a family of inputs equals the cleaner target's over the family's image under the connecting channel, and is at most the cleaner target's over any larger family\. Moving up the pre\-order can only raise the worst case\.

- 4.3 [`reductions/lem:linear`](../claims/lem-linear.tex): Linearity: post-processing, private settings and mixtures ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:linear))

  **Non-normative summary.** A squashing relation survives a stochastic post\-processing of both sides, and conditional relations with one common channel give relations for the weighted joint family and for its mixture\. A private setting therefore enters as part of the outcome and opens no block of the target space\.

- 4.4 [`reductions/def:joint-family`](../claims/def-joint-family.tex): Joint alphabet, classes and the joint family of a private setting ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:joint-family))

  **Non-normative summary.** A private setting with exact class weights joins the outcome in one joint alphabet, partitioned into one class per setting, and its conditional POVMs form one joint family with each weight inside the elements\. The family satisfies the class\-marginal identity and opens no target block\.

- 4.5 [`reductions/rem:intertwining`](../claims/rem-intertwining.tex): Classical maps before a squasher, and relabelling after one ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:intertwining))

  **Non-normative summary.** A noise or coarse\-graining map changes the physical family a squasher is built from, so it acts on the physical POVM before the first link\. A stochastic relabelling of both sides by the private setting after a link keeps the channel, the sectors, every pull\-back and every certificate\.

- 4.6 [`reductions/prop:pinching`](../claims/prop-pinching.tex): Sector pinching as a squashing ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:pinching))

  **Non-normative summary.** Pinching onto finitely many orthogonal sectors is a self\-adjoint channel, and a target whose elements are block diagonal for those sectors squashes to itself through it\. The pinching can therefore follow a squasher whose target and carried operators are block diagonal\.

- 4.7 [`reductions/cor:blockdiag`](../claims/cor-blockdiag.tex): Restriction to block-diagonal squashed states ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:blockdiag))

  **Non-normative summary.** If every target element and carried operator is block diagonal for a sector decomposition, the squashing channel followed by the pinching realises the same relation and makes every reachable state block diagonal\. For that channel the squashed analysis may be restricted to block\-diagonal states\.

- 4.8 [`reductions/prop:tensor`](../claims/prop-tensor.tex): Tensor products of squashing relations ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:tensor))

  **Non-normative summary.** Two squashing relations give one for the product measurement, through the tensor product of their channels\. Independent components can therefore be reduced separately and assembled without a fresh existence proof\.

- 4.9 [`reductions/lem:obstructions`](../claims/lem-obstructions.tex): Necessary conditions for a squashing relation ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:obstructions))

  **Non-normative summary.** If M squashes to F, the spectrum of each real combination of physical elements lies between the extreme spectral values of the same combination of target elements, and the physical span is no larger and inherits every linear relation with the identity\. A relation violating either is impossible\.

- 4.10 [`reductions/rem:obstructiondecide`](../claims/rem-obstructiondecide.tex): Exact decision of the necessary conditions ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:obstructiondecide))

  **Non-normative summary.** On declared numbers the spectral condition is decided exactly in operator form on rational enclosures, and the span condition by exact ranks and linear relations\. A loose decision would refuse more or less than the lemma says, which is safe: nothing emitted rests on the check having passed\.


### 5 Branching and flag targets

[Dependency view](group-05-branching-and-flag-targets.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Every measurement with a finite alphabet squashes to the flag PVM on its outcomes \([Lemma “Flag PVM as a top element”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:top)\), so the existence of a squasher is never the difficulty; its tightness is\. A generalized instrument followed by a squasher in each branch gives one squashing relation whose target stacks the branch targets as blocks, with each branch weight transported as an operator \([Lemma “Instrument\-conditioned squashing”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:master)\)\. Its instances cover a known mixture, a measurement block diagonal for an orthogonal decomposition and a register read by a POVM \([Corollary “Three special cases of instrument\-conditioned squashing”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:instances), [Theorem “Register\-conditioned squashing”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:mixture)\); common targets that merge the blocks again, flag\-extended targets and their rate cost, and the price of a general POVM on the register follow\.

- 5.1 [`reductions/lem:top`](../claims/lem-top.tex): Flag PVM as a top element ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:top))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every POVM with a finite alphabet squashes to the flag PVM on its outcomes through a measure\-and\-prepare channel, and to any PVM on the same alphabet that vanishes only where the POVM does\. A squasher therefore always exists; the work lies in finding a target tight enough to leave a key rate\.

- 5.2 [`reductions/def:generalized-instrument`](../claims/def-generalized-instrument.tex): Generalized instrument ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:generalized-instrument))

  **Non-normative summary.** A generalized instrument is a finite family of completely positive maps on one input system whose traces add up to the trace\. Each map is a branch, occurring with the probability its trace gives and leaving a subnormalized state on a system that may differ from branch to branch\.

- 5.3 [`reductions/lem:master`](../claims/lem-master.tex): Instrument-conditioned squashing ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:master))

  **Non-normative summary.** A generalized instrument followed by a squasher in each branch squashes to the direct sum of the branch targets, with or without the branch label in the outcome\. Branch operators are transported, and a branch weight is a fixed number only where its pull\-back is a multiple of the identity\.

- 5.4 [`reductions/rem:reading-master`](../claims/rem-reading-master.tex): The operator form of the branch-weight constraint ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:reading-master))

  **Non-normative summary.** The branch weights of [Lemma “Instrument\-conditioned squashing”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:master) \(iv\) are the only constraint the branching rule produces, and the operator form is the one to impose: it fixes each block's weight conditioned on everything Alice and Eve hold, while its trace alone admits a block index perfectly correlated with Eve\.

- 5.5 [`reductions/rem:master-scope`](../claims/rem-master-scope.tex): Branch labels as information the adversary may hold ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:master-scope))

  **Non-normative summary.** The branching rule files the branch label as a block index of the system handed to the adversary, so it models branches Eve may know\. A setting the measuring party draws privately belongs to the common\-channel rule of [Lemma “Linearity: post\-processing, private settings and mixtures”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:linear) \(ii\), which opens no block and produces no constraint\.

- 5.6 [`reductions/rem:branch-blocks`](../claims/rem-branch-blocks.tex): Conditionally characterized receivers under the branching rule ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:branch-blocks))

  **Non-normative summary.** The branching rule squashes a conditionally characterized receiver as soon as each branch squashes, stacking the branch targets as blocks\. A branch that cannot be modelled gets a block of its own, where the adversary fixes the outcome only in proportion to the branch's probability\.

- 5.7 [`reductions/rem:branch-probability`](../claims/rem-branch-probability.tex): The branch probability as an operator ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:branch-probability))

  **Non-normative summary.** In the branching rule only the branch label is classical: the probability of a branch is in general an operator, not a number\. That operator is what the squashed model retains about the branching, and it is the only constraint the rule produces\.

- 5.8 [`reductions/cor:instances`](../claims/cor-instances.tex): Three special cases of instrument-conditioned squashing ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:instances))

  **Non-normative summary.** The branching rule applies to a convex mixture with a known weight, to a measurement block diagonal for an orthogonal decomposition and to a register read by a POVM\. Only the known mixture fixes the branch weight as a number without further information\.

- 5.9 [`reductions/prop:common`](../claims/prop-common.tex): Common and dominating targets ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:common))

  **Non-normative summary.** If every branch target squashes to one POVM, the coarse\-grained measurement of [Lemma “Instrument\-conditioned squashing”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:master) squashes to that POVM directly, through the sum of the composed branch channels\. The branch blocks then disappear from the target\.

- 5.10 [`reductions/rem:dominating`](../claims/rem-dominating.tex): Dominated detectors and device families ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:dominating))

  **Non-normative summary.** A detector whose POVM dominates a characterized one is the characterized device plus a weighted flag, as in the published block\-resolved construction\. By [Proposition “Common and dominating targets”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:common), a family of devices whose members squash to a common target becomes one squashed model with the label a branch register\.

- 5.11 [`reductions/cor:flagsenough`](../claims/cor-flagsenough.tex): Arbitrary unpopulated block in a flag-extended target ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:flagsenough))

  **Non-normative summary.** In a flag\-extended target, the block the squashing channel never populates may carry any POVM, and the target does not depend on the device\. An uncharacterized branch can therefore be represented without a device model for it\.

- 5.12 [`reductions/rem:flagcost`](../claims/rem-flagcost.tex): Rate cost of a flag-extended target ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:flagcost))

  **Non-normative summary.** Replacing the unpopulated block by an arbitrary POVM gives a cleaner target, which hands the adversary more and costs rate\. What it buys is one target, independent of the device, for a branch that is unknown or adversarially chosen; an infinite\-dimensional branch takes the flag PVM\.

- 5.13 [`reductions/thm:mixture`](../claims/thm-mixture.tex): Register-conditioned squashing ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:mixture))

  **Non-normative summary.** A receiver conditioned by a POVM measurement of an incoming register squashes to the direct sum of its branch targets, with branch operators and branch weights transported as operators\. A branch weight constrains the squashed states only where it is a declared multiple of the side system's state\.

- 5.14 [`reductions/rem:povmreg`](../claims/rem-povmreg.tex): A general POVM on the register, and its price ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:povmreg))

  **Non-normative summary.** Keeping the register intact in the target would need a projective measurement of it; filing each branch as a block of the squashed system removes that hypothesis\. The price is bookkeeping only: a direct sum instead of a tensor product, the branch weight still transported as an operator\.


### 6 Event-level device facts, attack sets and the licence

[Dependency view](group-06-event-level-device-facts-attack-sets-and-the-licence.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** A device fact about a union of outcomes cannot in general be kept by a classical target \([Proposition “Obstruction to encoding event bounds in classical targets”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:obstruction)\), so it travels as a constraint entry, which is sound only on the reachable set\. The consuming engine restricts the squashed attacks to the admissible set the entries cut out\. The main result, the attack\-set licence [Theorem “The attack\-set licence”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:licence), proves that every squashed physical attack lies in that set whenever each carried operator's pull\-back satisfies a spectral certificate, in either source mode and, for party\-local entries, on a product measured system with a product squashing channel and each certificate on its factor's own channel\. The remarks say how the relation symbols read at the channel and the declaration level, what the licence does and does not license, and where its format is published\.

- 6.1 [`reductions/prop:obstruction`](../claims/prop-obstruction.tex): Obstruction to encoding event bounds in classical targets ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/prop:obstruction))

  **Non-normative summary.** Some three\-outcome qubit POVM with a lower bound on the sum of two of its elements has no squashing to a classical target that keeps that bound as an operator inequality\. A device fact about a union of outcomes therefore travels as a constraint entry rather than inside a flag target\.

- 6.2 [`reductions/rem:obstruction-scope`](../claims/rem-obstruction-scope.tex): Scope of the obstruction, and what it forces ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:obstruction-scope))

  **Non-normative summary.** A general quantum target encodes an event bound trivially; the obstruction concerns targets classical on the whole target space\. Such a bound travels as a constraint entry under the licence, and merging the outcomes is no remedy when they carry key information\.

- 6.3 [`reductions/def:attset`](../claims/def-attset.tex): Admissible attack sets in the consumer's format ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:attset))

  **Non-normative summary.** For a constraint list with real constants, the admissible attack channels are the round model's attack sets: adjoint conditions in each source mode and, for party\-local entries, a condition with a reference system\. The licence [Theorem “The attack\-set licence”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:licence) says when every squashed physical attack lies there\.

- 6.4 [`reductions/rem:relation-symbols`](../claims/rem-relation-symbols.tex): Relation symbols at the channel and declaration levels ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:relation-symbols))

  **Non-normative summary.** The consuming contract writes the relations operator\-valued at the channel level and as comparisons of real numbers at the untrusted declaration level; both spellings denote the same three relations\. Every entry this module emits carries a real constant and is admitted under either\.

- 6.5 [`reductions/def:licensed`](../claims/def-licensed.tex): Licensed entries and the party-local form ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:licensed))

  **Non-normative summary.** A constraint list is licensed when every right\-hand side is a real constant: untagged, as a multiple of Alice's declared marginal in trusted mode and a real number in untrusted mode, or party\-local\. A party\-local entry acts on one factor of a product measured system, against the marginal present\.

- 6.6 [`reductions/lem:party-local`](../claims/lem-party-local.tex): Party-local transport ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:party-local))

  **Non-normative summary.** For a product squashing channel, a spectral bound certified on one part's channel holds of every squashed attack as an operator inequality on all other systems, whose trace is the scalar entry\. Without the product structure the operator form can fail while the scalar form holds\.

- 6.7 [`reductions/thm:licence`](../claims/thm-licence.tex): The attack-set licence ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:licence))

  **Non-normative summary.** If each operator the squashing carries pulls back to a physical operator ≤, = or ≥ a multiple of the identity, every squashed physical attack respects the resulting constraint list in either source mode, factor by factor for per\-factor bounds\. Security against such attacks transfers with the same ε\.

- 6.8 [`reductions/rem:notlicensed`](../claims/rem-notlicensed.tex): Scope of the attack-set licence ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:notlicensed))

  **Non-normative summary.** The licence permits one thing, restricting the squashed attacks to the declared admissible set\. It licenses no trusted marginal, no constraint on Eve's side information, no data\-dependent or multi\-round constraint, no reuse under another proof technique, and nothing about the device\.

- 6.9 [`reductions/rem:gateweight`](../claims/rem-gateweight.tex): Weight of the licence in the consumer's gate ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:gateweight))

  **Non-normative summary.** In untrusted\-source mode, or where Alice's system has dimension one, the consumer's mechanical check of the licensed class degenerates and its gate rests entirely on the supplier's licence\. The licence is therefore a theorem with a named hypothesis rather than an attestation\.

- 6.10 [`reductions/rem:publishedlicence`](../claims/rem-publishedlicence.tex): The published squashed-attack-set format ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:publishedlicence))

  **Non-normative summary.** The adjoint format of the admissible set is the published squashed\-attack\-set format, and the published inclusion is the special case of [Theorem “The attack\-set licence”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:licence) \(i\) at one flag\-state operator with constant zero\. The licence generalises it to any certified carried operator in either source mode\.

### 8 The source-side reduction and the two-sided theorem

[Dependency view](group-08-the-source-side-reduction-and-the-two-sided-theorem.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Admissibility for a list of entries with real constants survives precomposition with any channel, in either source mode \([Lemma “Licensed admissibility survives precomposition with a source channel”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:precomposition)\)\. Replacing the specified signal ensemble of a trusted\-source specification by its virtual ensemble is itself an exact operational reduction, and the entanglement\-based description a source\-replacement certificate presents has the same full output \([Lemma “Exactness of the source map in trusted\-source mode”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:source-transfer)\)\. The main result, [Theorem “The two\-sided reduction in trusted\-source mode”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:two-sided), is that the source map and the squashing reduction then compose, in either order, into one exact operational reduction whose image lies in the admissible attack set, so security of the virtual squashed model transfers to the specified physical protocol with the same ε, against attacks that receive every signal in the first round and keep finite\-dimensional registers; every other attack is reached by a limit in the consuming engine \([Corollary “Security of the protocol and its net key”](../../meat-core/contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-meat-core/cor:composed-security)\)\. In untrusted\-source mode there is no source segment to transfer, and the receiver\-side reduction stands alone\.

- 8.1 [`reductions/lem:precomposition`](../claims/lem-precomposition.tex): Licensed admissibility survives precomposition with a source channel ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:precomposition))

  **Non-normative summary.** An attack channel admissible for a list of entries with real constants stays admissible when any channel is composed before it, in either source mode and for party\-local entries\. A Hermitian right\-hand side that is not a real multiple of the identity is not preserved\.

- 8.2 [`reductions/rem:precomposition-scope`](../claims/rem-precomposition-scope.tex): Scope of the precomposition lemma ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:precomposition-scope))

  **Non-normative summary.** The precomposition lemma needs only membership in the set the attack\-set displays cut out, at a separable input, and gives the inclusion of the specified\-source attack set, composed with any channel, in the virtual\-source one\. A general entry has no reading beside a non\-empty source segment\.

- 8.3 [`reductions/lem:source-transfer`](../claims/lem-source-transfer.tex): Exactness of the source map in trusted-source mode ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:source-transfer))

  **Non-normative summary.** In trusted\-source mode, replacing the specified signal ensemble by the virtual one is an exact operational reduction, and the entanglement\-based description a certificate presents has the same full output\. One source channel common to every letter is essential\.

- 8.4 [`reductions/rem:source-transfer-registers`](../claims/rem-source-transfer-registers.tex): The released signals as systems in transit ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:source-transfer-registers))

  **Non-normative summary.** The transmitted systems enter the first\-round attack as systems in transit, not as registers of the adversary, and the virtual one is finite\-dimensional\. A round\-by\-round attack with a memory of any dimension is reached from this type by a limit \([Lemma “Approximating an attack by all\-at\-once attacks with finite memory”](../../meat-core/contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-meat-core/lem:memory-lift)\)\.

- 8.5 [`reductions/rem:source-transfer-scope`](../claims/rem-source-transfer-scope.tex): Scope of the source-map lemma ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:source-transfer-scope))

  **Non-normative summary.** The source\-map lemma gives the specified\-source protocol no entanglement\-based description, uses no purification and asserts no equality of marginals\. With an empty source segment the two descriptions coincide, and parts \(i\) and \(iii\) of the lemma add nothing to the definition of one protocol\.

- 8.6 [`reductions/thm:two-sided`](../claims/thm-two-sided.tex): The two-sided reduction in trusted-source mode ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:two-sided))

  **Non-normative summary.** In trusted\-source mode the source map and the squashing reduction compose, in either order, into one exact operational reduction into the admissible attack set\. Security of the virtual squashed model thus transfers with the same ε to the specified physical protocol, against attacks of its type\.

- 8.7 [`reductions/rem:two-sided-scope`](../claims/rem-two-sided-scope.tex): Scope of the two-sided reduction ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:two-sided-scope))

  **Non-normative summary.** The two\-sided reduction relates no marginals of the specified and virtual descriptions and licenses no entry whose right\-hand side is not a real constant\. Its order\-independence concerns soundness; the order a chain records stays data\.

- 8.8 [`reductions/rem:ordering`](../claims/rem-ordering.tex): Recorded stage order, immaterial to soundness for licensed lists ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:ordering))

  **Non-normative summary.** Condition \(C3\) records the order of the source and receiver stages as data; for a list with real constants that order does not carry soundness \([Lemma “Licensed admissibility survives precomposition with a source channel”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:precomposition), [Theorem “The two\-sided reduction in trusted\-source mode”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:two-sided)\)\. A general entry is not preserved and is refused beside a non\-empty source segment\.


### 9 The number model and exact decisions

[Dependency view](group-09-the-number-model-and-exact-decisions.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Every number this module reads or emits, except the recorded emission displacement, is a binary64 value read as the dyadic rational it is, every declared or emitted operator entry and declared real scalar satisfies one declared lattice condition, and every condition on declared data is decided exactly; the section states the strengthening of exact admissibility this module decides \([Definition “The number model, the declared lattice and exact admissibility”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:exactadm)\)\. The decisions are taken in an ordered field that the contract leaves open, since no admissible choice changes a decision \([Definition “The arithmetic substrate”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:substrate)\)\. Positivity and operator\-norm bounds reduce to sign comparisons of characteristic\-polynomial coefficients \([Lemma “Exact decision of positivity and of an operator\-norm bound”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:exactpsd)\), and partial sums on the lattice are exact while the alphabet is small enough \([Lemma “Partial sums on the declared lattice are exact”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:latsum)\)\.

- 9.1 [`reductions/def:exactadm`](../claims/def-exactadm.tex): The number model, the declared lattice and exact admissibility ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:exactadm))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every number this module reads or emits, except the recorded emission displacement, is a binary64 value read as its dyadic rational; every declared or emitted operator entry and declared real scalar meets one lattice condition\. Exact admissibility here is the foundations core plus that condition\.

- 9.2 [`reductions/def:substrate`](../claims/def-substrate.tex): The arithmetic substrate ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:substrate))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every exact decision is taken in an ordered field F between ℚ and ℝ, with its Gaussian extension F \+ iF, and the field is left open\. No admissible choice changes a decision, every number a rule reads being rational, so ℚ always suffices\.

- 9.3 [`reductions/lem:exactpsd`](../claims/lem-exactpsd.tex): Exact decision of positivity and of an operator-norm bound ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:exactpsd))

  **Non-normative summary.** A Hermitian matrix with entries in the substrate is positive semidefinite exactly when, for every k, the sum of its principal minors of order k is non\-negative, and an operator\-norm bound reduces to such a test\. Both are decided by field operations and sign comparisons, with no eigenvalue computed\.

- 9.4 [`reductions/rem:psdalgorithm`](../claims/rem-psdalgorithm.tex): Algorithms that decide the positivity test ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:psdalgorithm))

  **Non-normative summary.** Any procedure that decides the positivity test of [Lemma “Exact decision of positivity and of an operator\-norm bound”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:exactpsd) exactly in the substrate conforms, such as the Faddeev–Le Verrier recursion or a rational LDL factorisation with the zero\-pivot test\. A procedure that decides it approximately does not, whatever its error bound\.

- 9.5 [`reductions/lem:latsum`](../claims/lem-latsum.tex): Partial sums on the declared lattice are exact ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:latsum))

  **Non-normative summary.** If κ \+ ⌈log₂ L⌉ ≤ 53, every partial sum of L entries on the declared lattice is a binary64 number and every addition forming it is exact\. A family on the lattice over such an alphabet therefore meets the rounds\-nowhere condition by construction\.

### 11 Exact emission

[Dependency view](group-11-exact-emission.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Where the exact construction of a reduction instance leaves the declared lattice, the emission normalizer declares a nearby datum, keeping the classes already on the lattice and snapping every other class for fidelity without reviving a zero row or column, or, where no element can host its residue, by a flagged last resort, and decides all six emission conditions last \([Definition “The emission normalizer, the declared datum and the declared model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:emitnorm)\)\. The main result, [Theorem “Exact emission of a reduction instance”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:emit), is that the emitted family is exactly admissible and squashes the declared model exactly, and that every claim of the instance holds of that model; where the normalizer moved the datum, nothing proved relates the two models, their distance least of all, and a run either evaluates off the snap or rests on a disclosed premise\. Which constraint entries are emitted and at which constants \([Corollary “The emitted constraint entries”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:entries)\), and how several measuring parties' emissions combine without a product datum \([Theorem “The emission of several measuring parties”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:emitted-product)\), follow; the remarks say what an emitted claim is about, where exact decisions stop, and why the emitted constraints are sound by theorem rather than by assumption\.

- 11.1 [`reductions/def:emitnorm`](../claims/def-emitnorm.tex): The emission normalizer, the declared datum and the declared model ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/def:emitnorm))

  **Non-normative summary.** The normalizer puts an exact datum back on the lattice class by class: it keeps the classes already there and snaps the others for fidelity, rounding to nearest and placing the residue only where support allows, or, failing that, by a flagged last resort\.

- 11.2 [`reductions/thm:emit`](../claims/thm-emit.tex): Exact emission of a reduction instance ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:emit))

  **Non-normative summary.** The emitted target is computed exactly, decided exactly admissible before emission, and squashes the declared model exactly; nearness to the handed model bounds nothing\. On blocks the record names as resting on [A\-SNAP](../../foundations/contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-foundations/ass:snapped-measurement), the run's conclusions about the device rest on that premise\.

- 11.3 [`reductions/cor:entries`](../claims/cor-entries.tex): The emitted constraint entries ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:entries))

  **Non-normative summary.** The emitted list is empty unless the request carries entries; then it holds the instance's entries, the class\-marginal entry where discharged and further entries whose certificate a claim decides\. Certificates hold of the emitted numbers, and off\-lattice inequality constants are rounded outward\.

- 11.4 [`reductions/rem:entry-tags`](../claims/rem-entry-tags.tex): Party-local tags and the one-factor emission ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:entry-tags))

  **Non-normative summary.** The licence covers a party\-local entry only through a certificate on its own factor's channel; a joint one can hold while the operator inequality fails\. On a one\-factor emission the untagged entry is implied by the party\-local reading, and rate is lost only in untrusted\-source mode at d\_A \> 1\.

- 11.5 [`reductions/thm:emitted-product`](../claims/thm-emitted-product.tex): The emission of several measuring parties ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:emitted-product))

  **Non-normative summary.** Several measuring parties are emitted as one exactly admissible artefact per party with a combination rule, never a product matrix\. The product target is a POVM squashing the product of the declared models, and every entry keeps its constant, scoped to its factor\.

- 11.6 [`reductions/cor:emitted-identity`](../claims/cor-emitted-identity.tex): The emitted family of the identity instance ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:emitted-identity))

  **Non-normative summary.** For the identity instance the normalizer moves nothing, so the emitted family is the handed datum itself, squashed by the sector pinching with no error term on an exact link\. A requested entry's certificate is the operator inequality itself, decided exactly on the emitted numbers\.

- 11.7 [`reductions/rem:emitclaim`](../claims/rem-emitclaim.tex): What the reduction's claim is about after an emission snap ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:emitclaim))

  **Non-normative summary.** Where the normalizer moves the datum, the claims are about the declared model, and nothing proved relates it to the handed one\. A run can evaluate off the snap on the reference companion; elsewhere a disclosed premise relates them\.

- 11.8 [`reductions/rem:claimscope`](../claims/rem-claimscope.tex): The claims that read a declared number ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:claimscope))

  **Non-normative summary.** The squashing, transfer, calculus and core instance results read no declared number; a submodule's instance reads declared data decided exactly, the licence exactly Hermitian operators and declared constants\. Where the normalizer acts, every statement is read on the declared model\.

- 11.9 [`reductions/rem:exactboundary`](../claims/rem-exactboundary.tex): The boundary of the exact decisions ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:exactboundary))

  **Non-normative summary.** Data without a finite presentation, the objects of an instance's hypotheses, and combinatorial data offer no numerical predicate: the first stay hypotheses, the second are settled by inspection\. The normalizer never touches the first, so the hypotheses hold of the declared model\.

- 11.10 [`reductions/rem:sound-and-atk`](../claims/rem-sound-and-atk.tex): Soundness of the emitted constraint list ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:sound-and-atk))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every entry this module emits holds on every reachable squashed state, as a conclusion of [Theorem “Composition of a chain”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:chain), [Corollary “The emitted constraint entries”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:entries) and [Theorem “The attack\-set licence”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:licence) and not as an assumption\. Inside the engine's security chain the whole list is covered by the stronger [A\-ATK](../../protocol-compiler/contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-protocol-compiler/ass:atk) through the engine's range lemma\.


### 12 The reference companion

[Dependency view](group-12-the-reference-companion.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** Where the declaring instance holds a reference of the measurement it snapped, the same instance run on the reference in place of the snap is again a reduction instance with the emitted alphabet, sectors and scalar values: its target is a POVM that the reference model squashes to exactly, with its own certified entries, known through enclosures formed from the reference's \([Corollary “The reference companion of a reduction instance”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:reference-companion)\)\. This needs only that the instance's datum, formed from any family with the properties of a reference, is admissible, which holds for the identity instance, passes through branching, and holds for the flat instance\. An entry built from the reference is emitted in binary64 by rounding its operator and moving its constant outward by a bound on the rounding, which keeps a certificate and enlarges the set the entry cuts out \([Lemma “A rounded constraint entry with its constant relaxed”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:row-relaxation)\)\. Where the instance forms its datum as a non\-negative combination of the blocks, as the identity and the flat instance do, factor enclosures of the reference's blocks pass to the companion's datum and target, columns side by side and cores scaled \([Corollary “Factor enclosures carried to the reference companion”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:companion-factor-enclosure)\)\.

- 12.1 [`reductions/cor:reference-companion`](../claims/cor-reference-companion.tex): The reference companion of a reduction instance ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:reference-companion))

  **Non-normative summary.** The same instance, run on the reference in place of the snap, or on its own snapped blocks where none is handed, is a reduction instance of the emitted shape that squashes that model exactly\. Its entries are enclosed from the reference, requested ones where their certificates hold there\.

- 12.2 [`reductions/cor:companion-factor-enclosure`](../claims/cor-companion-factor-enclosure.tex): Factor enclosures carried to the reference companion ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/cor:companion-factor-enclosure))

  **Non-normative summary.** Where an instance forms its datum as a non\-negative rational combination of measurement blocks, factor enclosures of the reference's blocks give one of each element of the companion's datum, columns side by side and cores scaled, and so of the target's block on that data sector\.

- 12.3 [`reductions/lem:row-relaxation`](../claims/lem-row-relaxation.tex): A rounded constraint entry with its constant relaxed ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/lem:row-relaxation))

  **Non-normative summary.** Rounding a certified entry's operator and moving its constant outward by a bound on the rounding's norm gives an entry that is again certified and that every attack and every state satisfying the exact entry satisfies\. An equality becomes two inequalities\.


### 13 Assumptions

[Dependency view](group-13-assumptions.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** These assumptions are the premises the reductions rest on and do not prove: a measuring party's device entering only through one POVM per round, the same physical measurement in every round with no memory across rounds, a supplier's artefact being its module's own output at an accepted version and true of the device, a measuring party's private setting being its own declared randomness, output\-state test statistics and declared constants\. The dimensions of the systems are the setting's, and the adversary's registers are finite\-dimensional as the round model's are; no assumption is made about the adversary's memory, which the consuming engine reaches at any dimension by a limit \([Corollary “Security of the protocol and its net key”](../../meat-core/contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-meat-core/cor:composed-security)\)\. No assumption about the distance of the declared numbers from a true operator family is made, because their exact admissibility is decided on entry; what remains a hypothesis is that the supplier's facts hold of the family declared \([Assumption “Supplier artefact whose claims hold of the device”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:recv), [Remark “What the supplier assumption is stated of”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:recv-scope)\), which for an exact, rigorously evaluated receiver artefact the run built itself holds under the receiver's own ledger \([Requirement “Omission of the supplier row for a receiver artefact the run built”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:recv-row-provenance)\)\.

- 13.1 [`reductions/ass:meas`](../claims/ass-meas.tex): Each measuring party's device as one POVM per round ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:meas))

  **Non-normative summary.** In every round each measuring party's device acts as one POVM: it emits a classical outcome, retains no quantum system, and everything after it acts on classical data\. It is the hypothesis about the devices behind the output\-state equality of the physical and the squashed protocol\.

- 13.2 [`reductions/ass:round`](../claims/ass-round.tex): Round homogeneity of the physical measurement ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:round))

  **Non-normative summary.** One physical POVM describes the measurement of every round, and each round's measurement acts as it describes whatever happened in other rounds: no detector memory and no drift\. Nothing is assumed about the attacks being independent across rounds\.

- 13.3 [`reductions/ass:recv`](../claims/ass-recv.tex): Supplier artefact whose claims hold of the device ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:recv))

  **Non-normative summary.** A supplier artefact read by a reduction instance is that module's own output at the accepted version it declares, and the device facts its claims state hold of the numbers exactly as declared\. It is the premise behind a reduction on supplier numbers, save an exact, rigorous artefact the run built\.

- 13.4 [`reductions/rem:recv-scope`](../claims/rem-recv-scope.tex): What the supplier assumption is stated of ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:recv-scope))

  **Non-normative summary.** [A\-RECV](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:recv) is stated of the exactly admissible declaration, not of an idealisation of it, and of one declared receiver\. No claim relates a key length from the declaration to one from the supplier's own floating\-point output; an exact, rigorous receiver artefact the run built has its row omitted\.

- 13.5 [`reductions/ass:probe`](../claims/ass-probe.tex): The station's private setting drawn from its own declared randomness ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:probe))

  **Non-normative summary.** A station's private per\-round setting is drawn from its party's own randomness with the declared class weights, independently of the adversary and the round's quantum input, never inferred from an outcome\. It lets the setting enter a joint outcome under one common squashing channel\.

- 13.6 [`reductions/ass:tsm`](../claims/ass-tsm.tex): Output-state test statistics ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:tsm))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every per\-round public choice of known bias at the measurement the emitted family describes, its private setting included where it has one, is a component of the emitted outcome and enters only through its statistics\. No derivation then assumes a state\-independent test fraction\.

- 13.7 [`reductions/ass:constdecl`](../claims/ass-constdecl.tex): Declared constants of the constraint list ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:constdecl))

  **Non-normative summary.** Every constant of an emitted constraint entry is fixed before the run by the device fact that certifies it and is no function of an observed statistic\. Observed data reach the analysis only through the acceptance set, which belongs to another module\.


## Part II: Engine requirements

### 14 Requirements: channel witnesses, post-processing and relations

[Dependency view](group-14-requirements-channel-witnesses-post-processing-and-relations.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** These requirements fix what the artefact carries: an explicit channel witness for every emitted link, the artefact's witness being the squashing channel followed by the sector pinching, and the carried tuple every emitted constraint is built from\. They also fix what the engine refuses: a relation with an error term, a noise or coarse\-graining map after a squashing link, a declared relation that fails a necessary condition, and a link of a reserved kind\.   **Non-normative summary.** Every link the engine emits carries an explicit channel witness, and a request naming a reduction that comes with none is refused\. Without a squashing channel the transfer of [Theorem “Security transfer from a constrained squashed model”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/thm:transfer) is not available \([Remark “Reductions that are not squashings”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:notchannel)\)\.

- 14.3 [`reductions/req:carried-tuple-emitted`](../claims/req-carried-tuple-emitted.tex): Emission of the carried tuple, and constraints built from it ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:carried-tuple-emitted))

  **Non-normative summary.** Beside each link's channel witness the engine emits the carried tuple of the link's squashing relation, and it builds every emitted constraint from an operator in that tuple\. Such a constraint belongs to the relation, not to one realizing channel\.

- 14.4 [`reductions/req:no-approximate-relations`](../claims/req-no-approximate-relations.tex): Refusal of approximate squashing relations ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:no-approximate-relations))

  **Non-normative summary.** A declared relation carrying an error term is refused, and no link is emitted with the status reserved for approximate squashing\. No theorem of this contract converts an error term into a security penalty \([Remark “Exactness of the reductions and generality of the transfer”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:why-exact)\)\.

- 14.5 [`reductions/req:round-homogeneous-emission`](../claims/req-round-homogeneous-emission.tex): Emission of the round-homogeneous case only ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:round-homogeneous-emission))

  **Non-normative summary.** The engine emits one declared model and one squashing channel for every round, built from the one physical POVM that [A\-ROUND\-SQ](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/ass:round) holds fixed\. A round\-dependent squasher, for which [Remark “Variants of the factorization”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/rem:variants) proves the factorization, is not emitted\.

- 14.6 [`reductions/req:post-processing-order`](../claims/req-post-processing-order.tex): Noise and coarse-graining before the first squashing link ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:post-processing-order))

  **Non-normative summary.** A noise or coarse\-graining map on the physical POVM is applied before the first squashing link, and a request to apply one after a link is refused\. The refusal reaches these two kinds of map and no other, so a relabelling of both sides after a link stays available\.

- 14.7 [`reductions/req:declared-relation-check`](../claims/req-declared-relation-check.tex): Exact check of a declared relation ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:declared-relation-check))

No eigenvalue is computed and no threshold is used\.

- 14.9 [`reductions/req:unpadded-branch-blocks`](../claims/req-unpadded-branch-blocks.tex): Branch blocks emitted as supplied ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/req:unpadded-branch-blocks))

  **Non-normative summary.** The engine does not pad the branch blocks of a composite target: a reduction instance emits the blocks its branches supply\. ## Part III: Flag-state and weight-preserving squashers

**Non-normative summary.** This submodule consumes a receiver artefact and turns it into a finite reduction instance\. It keeps the photon\-number blocks below a cutoff and replaces everything above by flags: the flag\-state target, which puts each outcome on its own flag, or the weight\-preserving target, which puts each outcome's certified high\-photon weight on every flag and one minus the total weight on its own\. Its first main result is that, whenever the certified weights total less than one, the physical measurement squashes to both targets, the two squashers compose exactly, and a weight the weight\-preserving target encodes holds on every squashed state \([Proposition “Flag\-state squashers: existence, chain and encoded weights”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/wpfss/prop:chain)\)\. The weight route first applies the dark\-count channel on the emitted alphabet, which requires it to descend through the coarse\-graining, then forms the joint family of the measuring party's private setting and routes the receiver's per\-setting multiclick weights into certified weights rounded down onto the lattice\. The resulting flat instance, on the encoded or the licensed route, is a reduction instance, and the second main result is its emission: the emitted family is exactly admissible and pulls back exactly to the declared model on the channel system, with its weights rounded down and still certified \([Corollary “Exact emission of the flat instance”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/wpfss/cor:emitted-flat)\)\. Where the emission moves a block below the cutoff, that statement is about the declared model, which nothing proved relates to the unrounded family; a run evaluates off the snap on the reference companion of the flat instance, or the moved class is disclosed\. Where the receiver is compiled at its normalized station, the instance is formed on that station and precomposed with the factored loss, which changes the channel witness and nothing else emitted \([Corollary “The flat instance of a normalized station”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/wpfss/cor:normalized-flat)\)\. The receiver's facts enter by named claims under A\-RECV, and the receiver assumptions those claims rest on enter the assumption closures with them\.

### 19 Flag-state targets and their chain

### 20 The weight route

[Dependency view](group-20-flag-state-and-weight-preserving-squashers-the-weight-route.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** The receiver supplies coarse\-grained blocks, a dark\-count channel on raw click patterns and per\-setting weights for raw outcome sets\. - 22.6 [`reductions/wpfss/req:fibre-equality`](../wpfss/claims/req-fibre-equality.tex): Certified sets equal to fibres of the coarse-graining ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/wpfss/req:fibre-equality))

- 22.7 [`reductions/wpfss/req:column-stochastic-refusal`](../wpfss/claims/req-column-stochastic-refusal.tex): Exact column-stochasticity of the declared dark-count matrix ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/wpfss/req:column-stochastic-refusal))

## Part IV: Domination links

**Non-normative summary.** This submodule relates two measurement families not by the size of their difference but by domination: one family dominates the other at one weight per sector\. Its main result, [Theorem “The noise channel of a domination”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/thm:noise-channel), derives from the branching calculus that the dominating family is then a noise channel followed by the dominated one together with classical flags, the channel keeping each sector with probability one minus its weight\. That relation is a typed link of its own, the domination link, placed on the target system after a squashing link, and a squashing link followed by it is an exact chain whose security transfers with the same ε \([Corollary “A squashing link followed by a domination link”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/cor:dom-chain)\)\. No requirement of the engine uses these claims: a snapped measurement is not related to its model by domination, and a run evaluates its key length on the model instead where it holds one\. They are kept for a declared device weight \(A\-DOM\), and the remark on rotated projective elements says why an idealised model needs its noise floor before it dominates a lattice family at a small weight\.

### 23 The domination link and its noise channel

[Dependency view](group-23-domination-links-the-domination-link-and-its-noise-channel.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** A domination link runs from a finite target family to a family it dominates at one weight per sector, its flags prepared into sectors added for the purpose or into indicator sectors the target already has\. The main result, [Theorem “The noise channel of a domination”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/thm:noise-channel), derives its channel from the generalized instrument that keeps or flags each sector and from the common\-target rule\. The closing remark says why a rotated projective element dominates no lattice element, so that a model needs its noise floor before domination can relate a lattice family to it at a small weight\.

- 23.1 [`reductions/domination/def:domination-link`](../domination/claims/def-domination-link.tex): The domination link ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/def:domination-link))

  **Non-normative summary.** A domination link runs from a target family G to a family F it dominates at one weight per sector, through a noise channel that keeps each sector with probability one minus its weight and otherwise measures a residual family and prepares a flag, in added sectors or in sectors F already has\.

- 23.2 [`reductions/domination/thm:noise-channel`](../domination/claims/thm-noise-channel.tex): The noise channel of a domination ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/thm:noise-channel))

  **Non-normative summary.** If G dominates F sector by sector, G squashes exactly to F with added flag sectors, or to F itself where F has flag sectors, through a noise channel that keeps each sector with probability one minus its weight\. The adjoint returns each sector projector scaled by that probability\.

- 23.3 [`reductions/domination/rem:rotated-projective`](../domination/claims/rem-rotated-projective.tex): Rotated projective elements and the noise floor ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/rem:rotated-projective))

  **Non-normative summary.** A projective element on a direction with no Gaussian\-rational vector dominates no non\-zero lattice element, and completeness then forces a weight of one, so no domination link runs from that block\. A noise floor in the model can remove it; after another channel it applies anew\.


### 24 Domination in a reduction chain

[Dependency view](group-24-domination-links-domination-in-a-reduction-chain.svg)

**Non-normative summary.** A squashing link at an exact datum followed by the domination link to another datum it dominates is an exact chain, so security transfers through it with the same ε and every entry is decided on the composite pull\-back \([Corollary “A squashing link followed by a domination link”](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/cor:dom-chain)\)\. No requirement emits such a chain; it is the relation a declared device weight would use\.

- 24.1 [`reductions/domination/cor:dom-chain`](../domination/claims/cor-dom-chain.tex): A squashing link followed by a domination link ([PDF](../contract.pdf#nameddest=claim-reductions/domination/cor:dom-chain))

  **Non-normative summary.** If an exact datum dominates another datum, the instance's squashing link at the exact datum followed by the domination link is an exact chain from the model at the exact datum to the other target with flags\. Security of that target transfers to the model with the same ε\.
