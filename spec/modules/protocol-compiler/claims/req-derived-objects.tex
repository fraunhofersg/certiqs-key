In the general format of Definition~\ref{def:declaration} the product
family, the \tref[lifted operator]{lifted operators} and the POVM an
\tref{ensemble form} denotes are defined by rules from declared data; what is
declared, and decided, is the data the rules read.

\begin{requirement}\label{req:derived-objects}
For a declaration in the general format of
Definition~\ref{def:declaration}: no entry of a product family
\eqref{eq:productfamily} is formed as a datum, rounded or decided on, here or
downstream; the \tref{lifted operator} of a scoped entry is never formed as a
datum; and the family \eqref{eq:ensemblepovm} that an \tref{ensemble form}
denotes is formed by no one and decided by nothing. Every exact predicate is
decided per \tref{measured factor}, on that factor's blocks and in that
factor's \tref{declared order}, and on Alice's data per Alice sector
(Definition~\ref{def:exact}). The engine refuses by type a presentation of a
\tref{choice-weighted family} indexed by a product alphabet
(Definition~\ref{def:weighted}).
\end{requirement}
