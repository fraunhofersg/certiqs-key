| `text` | The measurement of station 'bob', declared as a POVM. Declared in the run's protocol document (your own or a shipped template), not derived by the engine; you vouch for it. The premise is that these matrices describe the receiver you have, performed in every round on that round's system alone, whatever happens in the other rounds (A-SPEC).
